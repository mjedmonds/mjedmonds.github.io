ML engineer, roboticist, and builder at heart. I'm interested in domains with direct societal benefits via applied research. I want to build a world where AI works in service of furthering human flourishing, making basic needs affordable, and ensuring a healthy biosphere. Let's make the future as bright as possible.

I'm currently a Staff Applied Scientist at [General Motors](https://www.gm.com). Before that, I spent four years at [Cruise](https://getcruise.com) working on machine learning for behavior planning in autonomous vehicles. I received my PhD in Computer Science from UCLA under [Dr. Song-Chun Zhu](http://www.stat.ucla.edu/~sczhu/), with a focus on artificial intelligence, robotics, and cognitive science.
