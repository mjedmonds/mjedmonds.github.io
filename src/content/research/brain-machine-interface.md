---
title: Brain-machine interface for a robotic arm
description: Controlling a robotic arm with EEG signals.
period: "University of Dayton senior design project · August 2014 – May 2015"
order: 6
publications: [prince2015brain]
---
- Expanded the capability of a brain-machine interface built on EEG signals and a robotic arm.
- Developed an EEG signal classifier using Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA).
- Added six additional gestures, improving the universality of the interface.

**Collaborators:** Daniel Prince, Andrew Sutter, Matthew Cusumano, Wenjie Lu, Vijayan K. Asari (University of Dayton)
