---
title: Language for generalizable representations
description: How language helps embodied agents build interpretable, common-sense representations.
period: "Center for Vision, Cognition, Learning, and Autonomy, UCLA · March 2018 – present"
order: 1
---
- Created a virtual playground for embodied AI to learn interpretable, common-sense representations of its environment.
- Built a simulated environment in Unreal Engine 4 that couples language and vision in a scene graph.
- Devised a dataset of images, language labels, and object segmentation.
- Ongoing: a representation learning algorithm that uses language labels to decompose a latent encoding of the environment.
