- Built a virtual "escape room" to test causal generalization: surface-level features change from room to room, while every room is governed by a common abstract causal structure (a series of levers) that describes the actions required to "unlock" it.
- Ran human subject experiments verifying that human learners can discover the correct abstract causal structure.
- Built a hierarchical Bayesian model that performs similarly to human learners. The causal model solved the escape room while seven state-of-the-art model-free reinforcement learning algorithms failed.
- Result: both structural abstraction and feature generalization are critical for transfer learning and generalization.
