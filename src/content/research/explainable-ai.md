- Extended prior imitation learning work with human-understandable visual interfaces that describe the robot's haptic network and And-Or Graph.
- Showed that robots may need to "think" one way to complete a task but another way to explain their behavior: the explanations that best fostered trust were not the model components that best helped the robot achieve the task.
- Result: treat explainability as a first-class citizen when building AI systems that interact with humans.
